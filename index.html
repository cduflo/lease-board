Reading the numbers

  • $/mo assumes ~$3–6k due at signing, 10k mi/yr.
  • est. means no published 36-month offer — either a 24/39/48-month term converted, or 1.0–1.45% of MSRP for unlaunched models.
  • Cargo is max cu ft, all rear seats folded. Values with ~ are unverified.

CHEAPR reality check

  • $50,000 MSRP cap. Point-of-sale only, no retroactive claims.
  • The eligible list was last updated Sep 2025 — Ioniq 9, EV9, Optiq, Trailseeker and others aren't on it yet.
  • Dealer participation is voluntary; funds are first-come.

Not here on purpose

  • Discontinued: Acura ZDX, Nissan Ariya, Tesla Model X, Audi Q8 e-tron, Mercedes EQB, Polestar 2.
  • All Stellantis PHEVs ended with MY2026 — Wrangler 4xe, Grand Cherokee 4xe, Hornet R/T, Tonale.
  • Kia Niro PHEV gone. Crosstrek Hybrid is no longer a plug-in.
